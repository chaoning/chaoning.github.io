Research

My work centers on developing statistical and machine-learning methods to analyze large-scale genetic studies. I am particularly interested in methods that scale to biobank-sized cohorts and that reveal biological heterogeneity within diseases.

Scalable GWAS methods

Fast mixed-model and variance-component methods for association testing and heritability estimation in cohorts with hundreds of thousands of samples.

Disease subtyping

Genetic dissection of disease subtypes (e.g. type 1 vs. type 2 diabetes) using UK Biobank, FinnGen, and All of Us data.

Cross-cohort integration

Harmonizing GWAS across builds and cohorts to enable robust, replicable discovery and downstream analysis.

Method & software development

Turning methods into open, efficient, and usable software for the genetics community.
